The motion is
\begin{equation}
m\ddot{x}=-kx
\end{equation}

Multiply by velocity:
\begin{equation}
m\dot{x}\,\ddot{x}+kx\dot{x}=0
\end{equation}

The note prints the energy
\begin{equation}
E=\tfrac{1}{2}mv^{2}+kx^{2}
\end{equation}

and a decay time
\begin{equation}
\tau=b/m
\end{equation}

The frequency
\begin{equation}
\omega_{0}=\sqrt{k/m}
\end{equation}
